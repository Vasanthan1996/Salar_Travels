Questions

Frequently asked

What is the tempo traveller rent in Pondicherry?

The Force Urbania 16+1 costs ₹38 per km for single-day trips below 300 km. For longer and multi-day trips, the daily package is ₹6,500 per day plus ₹18 per km, with a minimum of 300 km a day. Toll, parking and permits are extra.

How many people can travel in the Urbania?

16 passengers plus the driver, with luggage space at the back.

Do you do Chennai airport pickup and drop?

Yes. We run Chennai airport transfers from Pondicherry at any hour. Send your flight time on WhatsApp and we schedule the pickup.

Are toll and parking included?

No. Toll, FASTag, parking, entry fees and interstate permits are charged at actual cost. Driver night stay is charged as per requirement, and GST as per the agreed billing terms.

How do I book?

Pick an open date in the availability calendar, fill in the trip details and tap "Book on WhatsApp", or call 95857 40154 or 97903 56335.

Is there any discount?

Yes. Use code SALAAR500 for ₹500 off outstation trips above 600 km.
